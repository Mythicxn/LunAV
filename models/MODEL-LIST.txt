LIVE2D MODELS
==============

hiyori   -> Hiyori
angel    -> Angel
traveler -> Traveler

To add another model:
1. Create models/<id>/
2. Put one *.model3.json and its referenced files there.
3. Restart the app; the model is discovered automatically.

Mouse tracking uses the original pixi-live2d-display model.focus(x, y)
behavior. Do not add a second requestAnimationFrame mouse tracker.
